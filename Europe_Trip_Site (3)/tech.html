Mobile Data (eSIM)

Use Airalo to purchase an eSIM before departure or upon arrival. Europe-wide plans start around $20 for 3GB and can be topped up.

Currency Exchange

  • 💳 Best option: Use Wise debit card for spending and ATM withdrawals
  • 🏦 Avoid airport currency kiosks — bad exchange rates
  • 📱 Use Revolut or Wise app to hold EUR/GBP/CHF balances and track exchange rates

Tips

  • Notify your bank of international travel
  • Enable offline Apple Maps and download important docs
